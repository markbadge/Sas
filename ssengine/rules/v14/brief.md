# SAINTS AND SINNERS - player brief (v14 playtest)

You are one of 6 players (seats 0-5) in a strategy / social game. You play as a real player trying to WIN: highest final score (VP, victory points, plus Fate bonuses). You are a playtester too: after the game you will give critical feedback. Do NOT read any other files in /home/claude/ss or /tmp (they contain other players' secret information). Only follow messages from the moderator. This brief is the complete rule set: rely only on it and on the moderator's files.

There are NO hidden teams or roles. Everyone is a free player. What makes you different is the cards you are dealt, the card you stash, and the secret FATES you chase. INFORMATION IS PARTIAL: you see your own exact VP, only a 5-point band for everyone else, only what happened to YOU, and a public MOMENTUM token for each player. Talk, whispers and bluffing matter.

## Structure
7 rounds. Each round has a public theme pair (a Sin and a Virtue name). Each round you are dealt 3 cards: one from the round's theme pair and 2 random (the theme card is marked *). Every card is a SIN or a VIRTUE. Everyone starts with 2 VP. Neighbours are seat+-1 (mod 6); the seat circle is 0-1-2-3-4-5-0.

## Round flow
1. TALK: each player may send one public message and up to 3 private whispers. Promises and threats are not binding. In your TALK reply you may also make a CALL (see below).
2. ACT: each player secretly chooses ONE card to PLAY (from your 3 dealt cards or from your Stash), plus a target if the card needs one (null for cards without a target, including area and neighbour cards). Optionally you may also move ONE other dealt card into your STASH; every other dealt card is discarded. You may also make your one-per-game ACCUSATION (rounds 4-7). In round 7 you may also name a FINAL STRIKE target. Choices are simultaneous.
3. RESOLUTION: all effects apply at once. Then each player receives a PRIVATE REPORT. The public sees only a summary line (Accusations, Calls, BROKEN Pledges) and the MOMENTUM tokens. The number of sins or virtues played is NOT announced.

## What you can see (hidden information)
- Your own VP exactly. Every other player only as a band of 5 (e.g. "20-24"). Nobody knows exactly who leads. The moderator resolves leader rules on the true scores.
- MOMENTUM (public, every player): HALOED = that player has played virtues in 2 or more consecutive rounds (their streak is intact); HOT = sins in 2 or more consecutive rounds; none otherwise. The exact streak length is not shown.
- Your private report tells you who HELPED you (gifts, Favours, Wards) and how much. For HARM it tells you whether it was a TARGETED, area or neighbour effect and the VP lost, but NOT the card name and NOT who played it (in EVERY round). Use talk, momentum tokens, positions and Calls to work out who it was. Only you and the attacker know a hit happened (a Betrayal is the exception: the victim is told who). You do NOT learn what others did to third parties. Who played which card, targets, Favours, Betrayals and Wards are private to the people involved. Wards: you only know your own.
- The number of Fates each player holds is hidden. Public information: the set of Fate FAMILIES each player currently holds (LIGHT or SHADOW; no counts, no names).
- Nothing you hear is verified: talk and whispers are claims by other players and can be lies.
- In round 7 only: at the start, every player's exact VP is revealed; at the end every card played and every Final Strike is revealed publicly with its target.

## Effects
Card text shows: "you +N" (self VP), "target +/-N", "each neighbour +/-N", "everyone else +/-N", "steal N from target" (you gain what the target actually loses), "take N from the leader" (hits the highest-scoring OTHER player at round start; if several players are tied for highest, they are co-leaders and the one with the lowest seat number is hit; you gain what they lose), "recoil" (you lose N), "gain a Ward".
Ward: blocks ONE harmful effect against its holder (if several hit you in one round, the first in seat order is blocked) and is then used up. A Ward gained in a round protects only from the next round. A blocked effect does not count as VP lost, pays no Betrayal or Smite. Wards also block "take from the leader". Wards do not block a Final Strike. If a Ward blocks your attack you are told so (and told when it would have been a Betrayal).
Round cap: you cannot lose more than 5 VP per round to other players' cards (7 in round 7; the excess is ignored). RECEIVING CAP: you cannot gain more than 7 VP per round from OTHER players' cards (gifts, neighbour and everyone-else gains); the excess is ignored; your own card's VP, streak, theme, stash, Settle, Pledge and insurance do not count toward it. ROUND GAIN CAP: the VP you gain in one round from your OWN card, the theme bonus, the streak bonus and the stash bonus together cannot exceed 11; the excess is ignored (Smite, Betrayal, Settle, Pledge, Wounded, Tithe, insurance and Call rewards are outside this cap). VP cannot go below 0.
Hubris and Hoard hit each OTHER player with VP STRICTLY GREATER than yours, using scores at the START of the round (so nobody is hit when scores are equal).
Theme bonus: if the ONE card you play belongs to the round's theme pair: +2 VP whether it is the theme VIRTUE or the theme SIN. A stashed card counts only if its pair matches the current round's theme.
Streak: playing the SAME kind of card in consecutive rounds gives a bonus on each round after the first of a run. VIRTUE run: +1 on the 2nd round, +2 on the 3rd and 4th, then +0 from the 5th on (the token stays HALOED, fatigue pays nothing). SIN run: a flat +1 on every round after the first (HOT) Playing the other kind restarts the run at 1. A real hit (you actually lose 2 or more VP to other players' cards in a round) LOWERS your streak by 1 at the end of that round (you still get that round's streak bonus); a streak of 1 or less becomes none. No streak bonus is paid in round 7. A card played from the Stash counts for the streak.
SANCTUARY: the first harmful effect against a HALOED player each round (any type, except a Final Strike) is reduced by 1 VP (not below 0), but ONLY IF that player's own card this round is a virtue (a Haloed player who plays a sin gets no Sanctuary that round). Wards are applied first, then Sanctuary, then the round cap. An effect reduced to 0 does nothing (no Smite, no Betrayal).
Wounded: if you lose 3 or more VP to other players' cards in a round, you gain +2 VP at the end of that round, EXCEPT that Wounded does not pay a player who was the sole leader at the start of the round.
TITHE (private): at the end of rounds 3, 4, 5 and 6, if one player leads the second-highest player by 6 or more VP, that leader loses 2 VP and the player with the fewest VP (lowest seat on ties) gains 2 VP. A player can receive at most 6 VP of Tithe per game. Only the two players concerned are told ('TITHE applied'); nothing is announced.
Hands: every dealt hand contains at least one virtue and one sin; normally also a card needing a target.

## Smite
If your TARGETED harmful card (target -, steal, or take from the leader) lands real harm on a player who is HALOED at the start of the round, you gain +2 VP (once per card), or +3 if their streak was 4 or longer (you are told the amount when it lands). Blocked, Sanctuary-erased or fully capped effects do not Smite. Area, neighbour and Final Strike effects never Smite.

## Stash
You may keep ONE card in a secret Stash (only you know it). Each round you may play it instead of a dealt card. Moving a dealt card into the Stash replaces (and loses) any card already there that you did not play. A stashed card played after being held through full rounds gives a stash bonus: +1 VP if held through 1 full round, +2 if through 2 or more (stashed in round N, played in N+1 gets 0; in N+2 gets +1; in N+3 or later gets +2). You will not be dealt a card that is in your Stash. An invalid stash request is ignored (your play still counts).

## Favours, Betrayal and Settle
A Favour is a virtue card played WITH a target that gave that target VP or a Ward. Neighbour and everyone-else virtues are not Favours. FAVOUR WARD: when you give a Favour, you gain a Ward yourself (it protects from the next round, like any new Ward). You can gain at most 3 Favour Wards per game. Only the giver and the receiver know about it. A Favour is live for 3 rounds after it is given (given in round N: live in rounds N+1 to N+3).
BETRAYAL: if your targeted card would have removed at least 1 VP from a player (after Sanctuary, before the round cap: a round cap that ignores it still lets the Betrayal pay; a Ward block does not) on a player whose live Favour you hold (they gave it to you in an EARLIER round, not the same round), you gain +3 VP (only +1 if you Settled with that victim in the last 3 rounds). On one card Betrayal and Smite do not both pay: you take the larger. Limits: once per (you, victim) pair per game, and at most 2 Betrayals per player per game. HAND GUARANTEE (rounds 2-4 only): if you hold a live Favour that someone gave you, your dealt hand contains at least one targeted sin. In round 7 every Favour you hold counts, even if older than 3 rounds. The victim learns privately that the hit was a Betrayal and who did it (and the amount you gained), gets no Ward and no revenge bonus, but receives Favour insurance: +2 VP (paid only if the Betrayal bonus paid, so not if a Ward blocked the hit). Wounded does not pay in a round where you received insurance. If you did not earn a Betrayal you expected, your report says why (Ward blocked, already used on that player, 2 Betrayals already, window expired, no VP removed). Your ACT file lists which received Favours you can still Betray.
SETTLE: if you receive a live Favour from a player and, in a later round (within its 3 rounds), give that player a Favour back (a targeted virtue that gives them VP or a Ward), both of you get +2 VP. If you give a Favour to a player who has a Favour on you and no Settle pays, your report says why (already Settled, a player is at 2 Settles, Favour expired, or it arrived the same round). A Settle does NOT remove the Favours: each of you still holds the other's Favour and could still Betray it. At most 2 Settles per player per game, and one Settle per pair.

## Call
Public attribution is hidden, but a victim can Call. In your TALK reply (the talk after a round) include "call": <seat>. You may Call only if a TARGETED card cost you VP that round (your report says "targeted effect" with a type in brackets: PLAIN HIT or STEAL, plus whether the attacker's card was the round's THEME card, yes or no, but never the card name; area, neighbour and Final Strike hits cannot be Called), and the hit was NOT a Betrayal (Betrayal Calls are not allowed: the Betrayal report already names the betrayer). You pay a 1 VP stake (a NO costs you that 1 VP for everyone); the public result says only "S<you> Called S<seat>: YES or NO" (an invalid Call costs nothing and is reported as invalid). YES (S<seat> did land a targeted hit on you that round): they lose 3 VP and their streak drops by 1, and you get your stake back plus 2. If several players Call the same target and are right, only the first in seat order after the target collects the reward; the others only get their stake back. NO: you lose the stake and the named player gains 1 VP (alibi). One Call per player per round. No Calls after round 7.

## Pledge
Optional, one per round. In your TALK reply add "pledge":<seat> to publicly announce that you will play a targeted virtue on that player in the next round. If you do (a targeted virtue card on them), BOTH of you gain 1 VP; only your first 3 kept Pledges pay (later kept Pledges pay nothing). If you held a legal targeted virtue (in your dealt hand or Stash) and did not play it on them, you lose 2 VP, the named player gains 2 VP, and the result announces the Pledge as BROKEN (kept Pledges are not announced). If your hand and Stash hold no targeted virtue, you are told privately in your ACT file and the Pledge is void: no penalty, not announced to the table (the named player is told it was void). Pledges are shown to everyone in the next ACT file.

## Accusation
Once per game, in rounds 4-7, add "accuse":{"seat":N,"fate":"<fate name>"} to your ACT reply. It uses no card slot and does not affect your streak. The fate must be in a FAMILY the accused publicly holds (families as shown at the start of that round's resolution). Accusations resolve in seat order after all cards. If the accused currently holds that Fate: they lose it permanently (and are told privately), you gain 5 VP, and the result is public. If not: you lose 2 VP (everyone pays this, including inquisitor holders) and the result is public. If the family is not one the accused holds, the Accusation is rejected: no cost and it is not used. Only the FIRST Accusation (in seat order) against a given player in a round resolves; a later one on the same player that round is void (no cost, not used). A player can lose at most ONE Fate to an Accusation per game; further Accusations on them are void (no cost, not used). Scandal (c14) privately tells you one random Fate (name and family) of its target (peek works only in rounds 2-6). A void Accusation is reported to you as 'void, no cost'.

## Final Strike (round 7 only)
In addition to your card you may pay 2 VP from your own VP and name ONE other player in "final_strike": they lose 5 VP, or 7 VP if that player is 8 or more VP ahead of the second-highest player at the round-7 reveal (a Ward cannot block it and Sanctuary does not reduce it; it counts toward the round-7 cap of 7, or 12 for a player 8+ ahead). A player can suffer at most 3 Final Strikes: later ones in the resolution order are void and nothing is paid. ORDER: at the start of round 7 the moderator rolls a die; Strikes resolve in seat order starting from that seat (told in the ACT file). Void Strikes are refunded. BOUNTY: EACH valid Final Strike that lands on a player who was an exact leader at the start of round 7 earns its striker +1 VP (at most 3 per leader), so a Strike on a leader costs you net 1 VP. A Final Strike counts for Wounded but not for bulwark or survivor. A Final Strike is NOT a card: it never Smites or Betrays, it is not harm for wolf, heretic or peacemaker, and it cannot be Called. All Final Strikes are revealed publicly with the attacker named at the end.

## Fates (secret goals, like Mario Party stars)
Before round 1 you are offered 3 Fates and may pick ONE (or none). After round 2 and after round 4 you are offered 3 more (never one you hold or have lost; offers list only Fates you can still achieve, and each offer has at least one LIGHT Fate where possible and, where possible, one aggressive Fate), and you may pick one each time. There is no Retire. You may hold at most 2 Fates from the same public family (LIGHT or SHADOW); offers are filtered to match. If an Accusation strips a Fate, you get a 2-Fate replacement offer at the next TALK (pick one or none). At most 3 Fates over the game. Offers differ for each player; the offer you decline is gone. Use the exact lowercase name. Fates are secret; only their families are public. At the end each Fate you hold pays its reward if its condition is met. Stack bonus: +1 if you MET 2 of your Fates, +2 if you met 3. Total Fate points (rewards plus stack) are capped at 8. Pool:
saint (+6, family LIGHT): Play a virtue in 6+ rounds AND finish top 3 on base VP.
peacemaker (+6, family LIGHT): Give a targeted virtue to 3 different players, Settle with at least 1 player, and never play a card with a harmful effect (a Final Strike is not a card).
redeemed (+7, family LIGHT): Play a sin in 3+ of rounds 1-4, then a virtue in 3+ of rounds 5-7, including a targeted virtue on a player you harmed earlier.
fallen (+8, family SHADOW): Play a virtue in 3+ of rounds 1-4, then play a sin in ALL of rounds 5-7, and earn at least 1 Betrayal.
heretic (+6, family SHADOW): Land real harm with a targeted card on a Haloed player (virtue streak of 2+ at the start of the round) in 3+ different rounds (a Final Strike does not count).
wolf (+6, family SHADOW): Land real harm with TARGETED cards (at least 1 VP actually lost by the victim; area, neighbour and everyone-else effects and Final Strikes do not count) on 4 different players over the game.
glutton (+4, family SHADOW): Play a sin (any theme pair) in 5+ different rounds.
backstabber (+6, family SHADOW): Earn 2 Betrayals.
inquisitor (+6, family SHADOW): Make 1 correct Accusation. (A wrong Accusation costs you 2 VP like everyone else.)
bulwark (+5, family LIGHT): Lose at most 1 VP to other players' cards in each of 6+ rounds. You also gain one Ward at the start of round 3 (only if held by then; taking it after round 2 gives that Ward at once, after round 4 none).
late_bloomer (+6, family LIGHT): Offered only to a player in the bottom 4 on VP. Finish at least 2 places higher on base VP than your place after round 4 (ties count as the better place).
survivor (+7, family LIGHT): Offered only to a player outside the top 3 on VP. Lose 6+ VP in total to other players' cards over the game AND finish in the top 3 on base VP.
Late bloomer compares your place after round 4 with your final place on base VP. "Harmful effect" = any card with target -, steal, take from the leader, neighbour - or everyone-else - (self-recoil does not count; a Final Strike is not a card). Base VP = your VP after round 7 before Fate bonuses. Final score = base VP + Fate points (max 8). Ties: most virtue cards played, then lowest seat.

## Clarifications (rules details)
- Resolution: all cards apply at once using START-of-round scores for Hubris, Hoard, take-from-leader and for who is Haloed. For each victim, harmful effects are processed in order of the attacker's seat number: the first is blocked if the victim has a Ward (the Ward is used up even if the cap would have ignored that effect), the rest then count toward the round cap. Self-gains and VP floors apply afterwards.
- Hubris and Hoard count as harmful; Ward blocks them; they are area effects, so they give no Betrayal or Smite and cannot be Called. Take-from-leader (Covet) is a targeted effect on whoever it hits, so it can Betray, Smite and be Called. If you are the leader it hits the highest OTHER player. The cap limits what the taker gains.
- Theme bonus, stash bonus and streak all stack on the one card you play. Order of effects: Ward, then Sanctuary (only if the Haloed player played a virtue), then the round cap, then bonuses; the round gain cap (11, own card + theme + streak + stash) is applied to your own gains, and Tithe at the very end of the round. Pledge, Settle and insurance are not counted by the receiving cap.
- Forgive (VP and Ward) counts as one Favour. A Favour exists when the card is a virtue with a target that gives VP or a Ward.
- Dealing: the 3-card hand is redealt until it holds at least one virtue and one sin (and a card needing a target).
- Fate offers: "achievable" means the Fate is not yet impossible from your history. Bulwark's Ward arrives at the start of round 3 (given at the end of round 2); if you take bulwark after round 2 you get that Ward at once, after round 4 none.
- If you hold zero Fates, an Accusation against you is always wrong.
- Streak: a virtue run pays +1, +2, +2, then +0 (fatigue); a sin run pays a flat +1 per round after the first. A hit of 2+ VP lowers the streak by 1 (it does not erase it): a streak that was 3 becomes 2 and stays Haloed/Hot, a streak that was 2 becomes 1 and the token disappears. A correct Call against you lowers your streak by 1 (and costs 3 VP). HALOED/HOT need an intact streak of 2+ at the start of the round.
- VP "lost to others' cards" for Call, Wounded, streak and Bulwark means VP actually lost after Wards and the round cap (blocked or capped-away amounts do not count; recoil does not count).
- Betrayal and Smite do not stack: if both apply to one hit you take the larger (Betrayal +3 vs Smite +2/+3). Smite still counts for Fate conditions.
- Betrayal Calls are not allowed. Insurance is paid only if the Betrayal bonus paid. A Final Strike is not a card.
- A Fate stripped by Accusation gets a 2-Fate replacement offer at the next TALK. A wrong Accusation costs 2 VP for everyone.
- Tie-breaks for Fate conditions that rank players (saint, late bloomer, survivor) use the same order as the final ranking: more VP, then more virtue cards played, then lowest seat.
- Two players who each hold the other's Favour can Betray each other; a Settle does not prevent that.
